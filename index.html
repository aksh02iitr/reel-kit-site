Good questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You talk to Claude in plain words. It runs every command, fixes setup problems with you and asks before it installs anything.

Which Claude plan do I need?

Any plan that includes Claude Code: Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise. Max is best if you make long reels often. It also works in Codex and Cursor.

How long does one reel take?

Usually 20 to 40 minutes from topic to MP4, and most of that is Claude working on its own. The render itself is a few minutes.

Do I need to show my face or use my voice?

No. Go faceless with the stack layout, use the Blip mascot, and pick a library voice. Or put yourself in it as a sticker with your own voice.

Does it work in Hindi or Hinglish?

Yes. Hinglish captions stay romanized and match the voice word for word. My YC reel on this page is Hinglish.

Can I make reels for clients?

Yes. Make as many as you like, for your own accounts or paying clients. You just can't resell or share the kit itself.

Is ElevenLabs free?

The free plan works for trying it. Free output can't be used commercially, so if your reels earn money, use a paid ElevenLabs plan (Starter is about $6 a month).

Will my reels go viral?

Nobody can promise views. The kit gives you strong hooks, tight pacing and clean visuals every time. What you say still matters most.

Is $6 the real price?

It's the launch price. It goes up to $24 after launch.

What do I get after I pay?

A download link to the full folder with a start-here guide. Your first reel takes about 30 minutes including setup.
